VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Acme Corporation ("Company") and Vendor Technologies ("Vendor").

§7 Payment Terms

The Company shall make payment to the Vendor within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

§9 Automatic Renewal

This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of one year unless either party provides written notice of termination at least 30 days prior to expiration .

§12 Limitation of Liability

The Vendor shall have unlimited liability for all losses, damages, claims and expenses arising from this Agreement .

§18 Intellectual Property

All intellectual property created during the provision of services shall be owned by the Vendor .

§21 Data Protection

Vendor shall maintain reasonable security measures for all Company data and shall notify the Company of any security incident.

§25 General

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements.
